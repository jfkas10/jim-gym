Install on iPhone

  1. Open the Jim Gym website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name Jim Gym and tap Add.
  5. Open the new Jim Gym icon from the Home Screen and sign in.

Your account determines which workout data appears, so two people can install the same app without sharing progress.
